How Trial works

A workflow built for the way hospitality actually hires.

Applications still come to you the way they always have — Seek, your website, walk-ins, Facebook. Trial doesn't replace your job board. It takes over from the moment an application lands.

01

Applications arrive on autopilot — however they come.

List a Trial-routed email on your online job posts, auto-forward applicant emails from your inbox, or display a Trial QR code at your venue for walk-ins. However an application reaches you — Seek, your website, Facebook, a CV at the counter — Trial picks up the candidate's name, contact details, and the role they're applying for. No forwarding clicks, no spreadsheets, no logins required.

02

The candidate receives a role-specific assessment.

Branded as if it's from your venue. Pre-filled with their name and the role they applied for. Front-of-house specific questions tailored to the role — beverage staff (bartender, bar back), floor staff (host, server, food runner, expediter), café roles (barista, café all-rounder), or management (bar supervisor, restaurant supervisor, duty manager, café manager, restaurant manager).

Not generic personality tests. Ten to fifteen minutes of the candidate's time, structured for the way you actually evaluate someone on the floor.

03

You see a structured candidate profile.

Their full assessment responses, an AI-generated summary, and a recommendation score calibrated for hospitality fit — not generic employability metrics. Sort, shortlist, and decide who's worth your interview hours. The score helps you prioritise; you make the call.

04

Progression tracked through to retained hire.

Interview, trial shift, hire — every stage in a single dashboard. Trial follows up at the ninety-day retention mark to confirm the hire stuck. Your hiring history compounds into a record that gets sharper with every decision you make through it.

Candidate data, handled properly

Your candidate data stays with you.

When an application arrives, Trial forwards the original email and resume to your inbox automatically — and deletes its own copy within forty-eight hours. We keep only what's actively used in the dashboard: structured assessment responses, a summary, and the journey state.

No candidate data hoard. No resumes sitting on our servers. Less compliance exposure for your venue, more trust from the candidates you're trying to hire, better-completed assessments as a result.

Founding venue program

A small group of operators, shaping the platform before launch.

We're onboarding founding venues in controlled waves through July and August. It's a deliberate choice — better to build with twenty operators who give us their honest read than launch loudly into a thousand venues we can't yet meaningfully support.

In return for being early, we ask for your operational reality — what's working, what isn't, what's missing.

  • Direct founder line Twelve months of direct access for setup, support, and product input. You ask, the founder answers — not a ticketing queue.
  • Real input on the roadmap What you ask for in the first six months gets weighed against every other founding venue's input — and shapes what we build next.
  • Controlled onboarding Personal setup of your venue, your roles, your assessment templates. Not a generic signup form — a deliberate, considered start.
