2.What Trial. is — and isn't

Trial. is a software platform. It helps hospitality venues run short, structured assessments to learn about candidates applying for front-of-house roles like waiting, bartending, hosting, or barista work.

Trial. is not a recruitment agency. We are not an employment agency, a labour-hire provider, or an automated hiring system. We don’t employ you, engage you, supervise you, roster you, manage you, pay you, recommend you, endorse you, or guarantee your suitability. The venue who invited you decides who to interview, trial, or hire — that decision is theirs, not ours, and not the AI’s.

Each assessment is for a specific venue and a specific role. We do not maintain a profile of you across venues. We do not show your information to other venues. Your data is deleted six months after your last activity (see section 8).

4.How the assessment works

  • A venue invites you by email or SMS with a link to your assessment, sent through Trial.
  • You answer a series of scenario-based, text-only questions on the role you applied for.
  • There is no video, no audio recording, no facial recognition, no biometric processing, and no personality test.
  • An AI system (currently Anthropic’s Claude Haiku 4.5) reads your written answers and produces a numerical score and a short summary.
  • Your responses, score, and summary are shared with the venue you applied to.
  • A real human at the venue decides whether to interview, trial, or hire you. The AI score is not a hire/reject recommendation — it’s just one input the venue may consider.
  • We don’t guarantee that the AI score or summary is perfect. AI can be wrong. A written assessment is one narrow window into a person — things like real-world workplace performance, how you actually work with people, your growth potential, and whether you’re right for a specific venue often don’t show up in writing. That’s for the venue to figure out through interview and trial shifts.
  • Completion of an Assessment does not guarantee an interview, trial shift, engagement, offer of employment, or ongoing work opportunity. Hiring decisions are made by the venue and are subject to the venue’s own processes.
