Privacy Policy.

Last updated 18 May 2026
Effective from 18 May 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Trial. (operated by Anders Berggren, ABN 71 441 417 792), and covers our pre-launch waitlist and Expression of Interest (EOI) form at hiretrial.com.au.

Important — limited scope. Trial. is not yet operating as a service. This policy covers only the personal information we collect via our EOI form and any subsequent communications with prospective venue partners. An expanded Privacy Policy will apply when our platform launches, and we will notify you before that change takes effect.

1.Who we are

Trial. is an Australian hospitality hiring assessment platform currently in pre-launch development. We are based in Australia and operate under Australian law, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

If you need to contact us about your personal information, email privacy@hiretrial.com.au.

2.What we collect

When you submit our EOI form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your venue name
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • Your venue location / suburb (optional)
  • Any business-related information or free-text notes you choose to share

We may also automatically collect technical information when you visit our website:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent

We do not collect: passwords, financial information, sensitive information (as defined under the Privacy Act), or any data about job candidates or hiring decisions — because we are not yet running our service.

3.Why we collect it

We collect your information for the following purposes:

  • To add you to our pre-launch waitlist
  • To send you updates about Trial.'s launch timeline and product progress
  • To contact you about founding partner pricing and beta participation
  • To respond to questions or feedback you send us
  • To improve our pre-launch communications

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not use it for any purpose other than those listed above.

4.How we store it

Your information is stored using the following third-party providers:

  • Supabase (APAC-hosted infrastructure) — for waitlist records
  • Resend (email service provider) — for sending you communications
  • Vercel (web hosting) — handles your form submission

These are reputable platforms with their own privacy obligations. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS), apply access controls, and access is limited to authorised personnel only.

Some of these providers may store or process data outside Australia. By submitting your information, you consent to this cross-border transfer for the limited purposes described above.

While we take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide, no method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will respond promptly to any incident that could affect your personal information.

5.How long we keep it

We keep your EOI information until:

  • You ask us to delete it (see Section 7), or
  • 12 months after your last interaction with us if you haven't engaged further, or
  • We launch and you decline to become a customer, after which we delete it within 60 days

6.Marketing communications

If you give us your email via the EOI form, we may send you launch updates and product news. Every email we send will include a clear unsubscribe link, in line with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth). If you unsubscribe, we will stop sending marketing emails within a reasonable timeframe — though we may still contact you about specific things you've asked us about.

7.Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information we hold
  • Correct it if it's wrong
  • Delete it (within reasonable limits — e.g., we may retain records we're required to keep by law)
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time
  • Complain to us first, and to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you're not satisfied with our response

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@hiretrial.com.au with your request. We aim to respond within 14 days.

8.Cookies and automated protections

Our website uses minimal cookies — only those required for the site to function and basic anonymous analytics. We do not use cookies for advertising or third-party tracking at this pre-launch stage.

We may use automated spam and abuse prevention tools to help protect our website and forms from malicious activity.

9.Children's privacy

Trial. is a business-to-business platform. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we've accidentally collected information from a minor, please contact us and we will delete it.

10.Changes to this policy

We will update this Privacy Policy when:

  • We launch our full platform (significantly expanded scope and obligations)
  • Any of the practices described here change

If we update it, we will post the new version here and update the "Last updated" date at the top. For substantive changes — particularly the launch transition — we will email anyone on our EOI list before the new policy takes effect.

11.Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia.
